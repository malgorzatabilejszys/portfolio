Ergonomic Workstation Design

A comprehensive redesign of a traditional workstation to improve ergonomics, productivity, and user comfort.

Client

OfficePro Solutions

Timeline

6 months (January - June 2023)

My Role

Lead Designer, UX Researcher

View on the company's Website View Technical Documentation
01

The Challenge

Modern office workers spend an average of 8+ hours daily at their workstations, leading to increased reports of repetitive strain injuries, back problems, and decreased productivity. The challenge was to design a workstation that addresses these issues while being cost-effective to manufacture and visually appealing.

02

My Approach

I employed a user-centered design methodology, combining engineering principles with UX research:

  • Conducted extensive user research with 50+ office workers to identify pain points
  • Created multiple prototypes using CAD software for precise measurements
  • Developed an interactive digital interface for height adjustment and posture monitoring
  • Optimized the design for manufacturing efficiency and cost-effectiveness
  • Implemented user testing protocols to validate design decisions
03

The Solution

The final design features a modular workstation with:

  • Motorized height adjustment with programmable presets
  • Integrated cable management system
  • Customizable work surface configurations
  • Smart connectivity with mobile app for posture reminders
  • Sustainable materials with 60% recycled content

Results

The redesigned workstation led to:

  • 15% increase in productivity during extended work sessions
  • Manufacturing cost reduction of 8% compared to previous models
  • Positive user feedback on both functionality and aesthetics
